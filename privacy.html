Privacy policy

yt-agent is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uses Google OAuth to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload) and to save preview files to the owner's own Google Drive (scope drive.file).

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person. OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment and are never shared with third parties. Google user data is used solely to upload the owner's own videos and files.
